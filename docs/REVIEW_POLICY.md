# Evidence review policy

INQUISITION is a curated retrospective casebook. It is not a live intelligence feed.

## Review cadence

- Before a public release, open each case's most consequential primary source and confirm that the summary still matches the artifact.
- Quarterly, sample the issuer, regulator, and litigation links for link rot.
- Review a case when a user reports a broken or misleading link or when a cited public record materially changes.

## Source hierarchy

1. Issuer filings and directly published company material
2. Regulators and government agencies
3. Litigation indexes and court artifacts
4. Search or navigation hubs
5. News and trade reporting for corroboration

Reporting may establish chronology or context, but it should not be the sole support for a legal or financial conclusion.

## Financial context

Use a cited filing where practical. Mark rounded or synthetic numbers as illustrative. Public company financials provide scale; they are not automatically cyber-loss figures.

## Editorial boundary

The casebook teaches decomposition, evidence discipline, and assumption testing. It does not determine legal materiality or provide legal, investment, actuarial, insurance, or incident-response advice.
